A FIELD GUIDE

Make an orbit.
Keep the light.

  1. Curve around a sun. Hold left/right or drag on the flight area to steer. You always move forward.
  2. Cross your own wake. The loop captures the suns inside. Two suns together earn four times the light.
  3. Keep going. Each sun adds 3 seconds. Capture again within 9 seconds to grow your chain, up to ×4.

Red bodies and the edge cost 5 seconds. Near misses recharge your surge. Hold Space or Surge for speed and wider orbits.
