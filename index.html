How to fly

  1. Curve around a sun. Hold left or right, or drag on the flight area, to steer. You always move forward.
  2. Cross your own wake. The loop captures every sun inside it. Two suns in one loop earn four times the light.
  3. Keep going. Each sun adds 3 seconds. Capture again within 9 seconds to grow your chain, up to ×4.

Red bodies and the edges cost 5 seconds. Near misses recharge your surge. Hold Space or Surge for speed and wider orbits.
